# My lil Drums

A drum sequencer with sixteen sample pads for VCV Rack 2. Paint beats on a piano roll, shape each pad with its own
envelope, filter and compressor, and play the result out as a stereo mix, as sixteen separate outs, or as MIDI.
By A_Development.

## Pads and the starter kit

Sixteen pads in a 4x4 grid, pad 1 bottom-left. Pads 1-8 load a small starter kit that is synthesised for this plugin
(no sampled material): kick, snare, closed hat, open hat, clap, rim, tom, crash. Pads 9-16 start empty.
Load your own with right-click, *Load WAV into the selected pad...* (16/24-bit PCM or 32-bit float WAV, mono or stereo).

## Sequencer

- Piano roll: 16 pad rows, 16 steps per page, up to 64 steps in four pages (the tabs under the roll).
- Click a cell to write a hit, click a lit cell (or right-click) to erase, drag to paint or erase along a row.
- Click a row number to select that pad and hear it. Clicking a pad in the grid does the same. Writing a cell also
  selects its row.
- The velocity lane under the roll shows the selected pad's steps for the current page; drag over a lit step to set
  its velocity.
- 16 pattern slots (PATTERN knob), each with its own length (STEPS knob). BPM and SWING are on the panel.
- CLOCK input: one pulse per step; with it patched the internal clock is off. RESET restarts the pattern. RUN is a
  latching button plus a gate input.

## Pad editor

The knobs on the right follow the selected pad: VOL, PAN, PITCH (+-12 semitones), ATK, DEC, SUS, REL (ADSR),
LP and HP filters, compressor (THRESH, RATIO, C ATK, C REL, MAKEUP) and CHOKE.

- GATE button: with it on, the pad is released after one step; with it off, the sample plays to its end.
- CHOKE: pads that share a group number (1-8) cut each other off, as an open hat is cut by a closed hat. 0 is no group.

## Outputs

OUT L / OUT R carry the stereo mix (LEVEL knob). Each pad also has its own mono out (pad volume applied; the same signal
is still in the mix).

## Right-click menu

- Load WAV into the selected pad, clear the selected pad.
- Kits: *Save kit as...* writes the sixteen sample paths and pad settings to a JSON file (default folder is
  `MyLilDrums/kits` in Rack's user folder); saved kits are listed below it to load again.
- Factory beat into this pattern: Boom bap, House, Trap (replaces the pattern and sets it to 16 steps).
- Clear the current pattern.
- MIDI out: pick a driver and device. Each hit sends note 36 + pad (pad 1 = 36) on channel 10 with the step's velocity;
  the note-off follows half a step later.

## Saving

Patterns, sample paths (the starter kit is stored relative to the plugin), pad settings and the MIDI device are saved
with the patch.

## Building

Rack SDK 2.6.6 and MSYS2 (Windows) or a Unix shell: `make` builds, `make test` runs the core checks, `make dist` packs
the plugin. On Windows from PowerShell: `pwsh -File tools/mk.ps1 test`. Panel art: `py -3.11 tools/panel.py`
(needs fontTools) writes `res/` and `src/layout.hpp`. The starter kit is regenerated by `tools/gen_kit.py`.
